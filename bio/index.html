About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Illinois Institute of Technology, advised by Farzaneh Derakhshan. I work on programming languages and formal methods, with a focus on separation logic, program verification, and compiler security.

My recent work develops LabelBI, a logic based on the logic of Bunched Implications that enables compositional reasoning about underapproximations. I also work on logical frameworks for compiler validation and static performance analysis frameworks on Processing-in-Memory (PIM) systems.

Before my Ph.D., I spent five years as a software engineer and engineering lead in industry, building backend systems at startups and at Tencent.
