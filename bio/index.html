About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Illinois Institute of Technology, advised by Farzaneh Derakhshan and Minxuan Zhou. I work on programming languages and formal methods.

My recent work develops LabelBI, a logic based on the logic of Bunched Implications that enables compositional reasoning about underapproximated analyses. I also work on logical frameworks for compiler validation and static performance analysis frameworks on Processing-in-Memory (PIM) systems.

Before my Ph.D., I spent five years as a software engineer and engineering lead in industry, building backend systems at two startups and Tencent. I received my B.S. in Civil Engineering from Central South University in 2017.
